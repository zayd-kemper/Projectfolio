# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ProjectFolio is for engineering and science students, early-career technical candidates, and teams who need to document projects clearly enough for recruiters, reviewers, collaborators, and application platforms. Users are usually collecting proof of work, polishing public portfolio pages, managing project media, reviewing analytics, or publishing work to a professional network-style feed.

## Product Purpose

ProjectFolio helps technical users turn project work into credible public portfolios, shareable project pages, team profiles, exports, and discoverable professional activity. Success means a user can build trust quickly: visitors understand what was built, what role the user played, what evidence supports the work, and how to contact or evaluate them.

## Brand Personality

Professional, engineering-minded, and quietly polished. The interface should feel capable and trustworthy without becoming sterile, decorative, or generic.

## Anti-references

Avoid cheap emoji-driven iconography, AI-purple gradients, oversized rounded cards, generic three-card marketing grids, vague startup copy, decorative fake dashboards, cluttered feed layouts, and "vibe-coded" embellishments that do not help the user's task. The discover page should feel closer to a credible professional network than a rudimentary project directory.

## Design Principles

1. Make evidence easy to scan: project outcomes, roles, media, metrics, and links should surface without forcing users through decoration.
2. Keep product surfaces calm and task-first: navigation, settings, editors, and analytics should prioritize clarity, density, and predictable controls.
3. Let public pages feel authored: portfolio and team pages should support customization while preserving strong defaults and readable structure.
4. Use engineering craft as the visual language: precise spacing, consistent iconography, restrained color, and real interaction states.
5. Build for scale and trust: loading, empty, error, dark mode, accessibility, and long-content cases should feel deliberate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-friendly interaction across app and public surfaces. Respect reduced motion, preserve visible focus states, avoid placeholder-only form labeling, and keep dark mode readable without relying on color alone.
